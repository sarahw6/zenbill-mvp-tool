🏛 Patient-Provider Dispute Resolution (PPDR)
When to use: Bill exceeds Good Faith Estimate by >$400 · Self-pay or uninsured
Legal basis: No Surprises Act, 45 CFR § 149.620
1
Request itemized bill and compare to GFE line by line
2
Pay the GFE amount as the undisputed balance
3
Submit PPDR dispute to CMS within 120 days of receiving bill
4
Independent arbitrator issues binding decision

📷 Step 1 — OCR
Extract from bills:
  • Provider name, NPI, address
  • Patient name, DOB, account #
  • CPT / HCPCS codes + units + charges
  • Revenue codes (facility bills)
  • Diagnosis codes (ICD-10)
  • Insurer name, claim #, EOB
  • GFE amount (if present)
Recommended: AWS Textract ($1.50/1k pgs) · Google Document AI
🤖 Step 2 — AI Analysis
  • CPT Validation
  • Price Benchmarking (vs. Medicare)
  • Unbundling Detection (NCCI edits)
  • Duplicate Detection
  • GFE Comparison (line-by-line)
  • No Surprises Act Check
  • Charity Care Eligibility (FPL%)
  • Dispute Path Scoring
🗄 Step 3 — Data Sources
Free / Public:
  • CMS PFS RVU File (Medicare rates)
  • CMS NCCI Edit Tables
  • MEPS 2024 Inpatient Survey
  • SPARCS 2024 (NY)
Paid / Licensed:
  • FAIR Health (commercial claims)
  • Turquoise Health (negotiated rates)
  • AMA CPT codes (full set)
